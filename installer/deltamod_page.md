# DELTARUNE Online

**AI disclosure:** the code of this mod was written with AI assistance (Claude, using Anthropic's Claude Code), directed and play-tested by traebon. Based on DELTARUNE Multiplayer by AeEn123AI (GPL-3.0).

Online co-op for **all of DELTARUNE (Chapters 1 to 5)** for 2 to 8 players. Every player runs the game on their own PC and controls their own party member.

- **Pick your party member:** Kris, Susie, Ralsei or Noelle. In battle you choose their actions and press for their attacks.
- **Everyone dodges** with their own SOUL. Your grazes add to the shared TP bar.
- **Explore on your own** and see each other with name tags.
- **Open-world battles:** fight what you touch, or walk up to a fighting friend and press Z to join.
- **Shared story:** cutscenes play for everyone at the same time.
- **Proximity voice chat** (push to talk: V).

## How to play together

1. Everyone starts the game with this mod and picks the **same chapter**.
2. Press **F9** for the multiplayer menu: set your name and character.
3. The host chooses **Host a game** (needs UDP port 26510 forwarded, or use Radmin VPN, ZeroTier or Tailscale).
4. Everyone else types the host's address and chooses **Join game**.

Everyone needs the **same mod version**. Players using this modpack can play with players using the DELTARUNE Online launcher.

The mod keeps its own save files (in %LOCALAPPDATA%\DELTARUNE_ONLINE), so your normal saves are untouched.

This modpack is made for the current Steam version of DELTARUNE. If DELTARUNE updates, wait for an updated modpack, or use the launcher from the GameBanana page, which patches any version itself and updates automatically.
