DELTARUNE Online: play DELTARUNE together over the internet.

You need your own copy of DELTARUNE on Steam. This download contains no game files.

INSTALL
Double-click "DELTARUNE Online.exe" and press INSTALL. It copies your game to a separate
folder and patches the copy (about 1 GB, a few minutes). Your Steam copy and normal saves
are never changed. Afterwards, start it from the "DELTARUNE Online" shortcut on your desktop.
If Windows SmartScreen warns about an unknown app, that's because this isn't signed:
choose More info, then Run anyway, only if you trust the person who sent you this.

UPDATES
Every time you open DELTARUNE Online it checks for a new version and installs it by itself.
It also patches the game again by itself when Steam updates DELTARUNE.

PLAY
1. Everyone opens DELTARUNE Online, picks the same chapter, and starts or loads a file.
2. Press F9 in game. Controls & info and Settings are in that menu.
3. The host chooses "Host a game". The host needs UDP port 26510 open on their router
   (or use a program like Hamachi, which needs no router change).
4. Everyone else sets "Host address" to the host's address, then chooses "Join game".

SAVES
This mod keeps its own save files in %LOCALAPPDATA%\DELTARUNE_ONLINE, so you start fresh.
To bring in your normal saves, copy the files from %LOCALAPPDATA%\DELTARUNE into that folder.

UNINSTALL
Open DELTARUNE Online and click Uninstall at the bottom. Your mod saves are kept.
